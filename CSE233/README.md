# CSE233 - Circuits and Electronics Laboratory

This repository contains the lab experiments for the CSE233 - Circuits and Electronics Laboratory course. The course applies the theory of circuit analysis in practice: each experiment is first calculated by hand and simulated in the [Falstad circuit simulator](https://www.falstad.com/circuit/), then built on a breadboard with real components and measured with a multimeter, function generator, and oscilloscope.

## Course Description

The "Circuits and Electronics Laboratory" (CSE 233) is a compulsory, first-cycle undergraduate course offered in the fall semester of the second year. It carries 2 ECTS credits and aims to reinforce the material taught in lectures and to illustrate measurement and analysis techniques. The course covers electrical measurement, resistor color codes, Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, voltage and current dividers, the Thevenin and Norton theorems, RC and RL circuits, rectifiers, transistors, and op-amps.

## Course Summary

### Topics Covered

- Electrical measurement and laboratory equipment
- Resistor, inductance, and capacitance color codes
- Ohm's Law and Kirchhoff's Laws
- Voltage and current divider circuits
- Superposition and multiplicativity theorems
- Thevenin, Norton, and maximum power transfer theorems
- RC and RL circuits
- Diodes and rectifiers

### Grading

- **Lab Experiments (6 labs × 10%):** 60%
- **Final Exam:** 40%

### Workflow of Each Lab

1.  **Preparation:** The circuits are calculated by hand and simulated in Falstad before coming to the lab.
2.  **Experiment:** The circuits are built on a breadboard and measured in the lab.
3.  **Comparison:** The measured values are compared with the calculated and simulated values.
